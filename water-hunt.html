Mosquito Water Hunt

Mosquitoes need still water to grow. Find it, dump it, and they never hatch.

Name
Date

How to play

  1. Take a grown-up with you and walk around outside.
  2. Look for water sitting still. Even a bottle cap counts.
  3. Tick the box each time you find one. Write the number you found.
  4. Ask your grown-up to dump it out and turn it upside down.
  5. Add up your points at the bottom, then do it again next week.

What to look for 1 point each

  • Bucket or pail
  • Old tire
  • Flower pot saucer
  • Bird bath
  • Pet water bowl
  • Kiddie pool
  • Toy left outside
  • Wheelbarrow
  • Fold in a tarp
  • Clogged rain gutter
  • Trash can lid
  • Puddle that stays
  • Watering can
  • Boat cover
  • Stock tank
  • Something surprising

Bonus points

  • You spotted wigglers moving in the water +5
  • You found water a grown-up walked right past +5
  • You checked again a week later +10

Rules — read these first

  • Stay away from creeks, sloughs, dugouts and ponds unless a grown-up is with you.
  • Do not drink, splash in, or play in any of the water you find.
  • Let grown-ups handle mosquito dunks and bug spray.
  • Stay out of other people's yards unless they invited you.
